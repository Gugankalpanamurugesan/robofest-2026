NOT JUST
ROBOTS. NEW

ROBOFEST is a celebration of curiosity, engineering and the people who turn bold ideas into working machines.

Presented here as a student landing-page concept for ROBOTiX Club, NIT Raipur, this experience invites makers, problem-solvers and future engineers to explore robotics through challenges, collaboration and hands-on learning.
